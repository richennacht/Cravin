Cravin

A private meeting copilot for your desktop. It listens, translates and suggests answers, and it runs locally by default.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit

Both sides of the call

Follows you and the other side as the meeting happens.

Translate mid-sentence

Anything not in your language shows up translated, live.

Local by default

Speech models run on your machine. Nothing leaves it unless you say so.

Stays out of shares

The assist overlay is hidden from most screen shares.

First launch

Cravin isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. After that, Cravin updates itself.

Early preview. Some features are still in progress. Built on Handy, MIT licensed.
